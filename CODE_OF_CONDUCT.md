# Community code of conduct

PixelLilt Studio welcomes constructive, respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and skill levels.

Be considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. Focus on the technical question, credit others' work, and refrain from harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or spam. Do not post secrets, personal images, or private vulnerability details in public discussions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove disruptive comments and restrict participation when needed. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for platform safety concerns. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use private reporting. For other conduct concerns, request a private communication channel rather than exposing personal details in a public issue.
