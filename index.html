    03 / THE SMALL PRINT

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    Do you upload my images anywhere?+

    No. File reading and conversion run locally with browser image and canvas APIs. No analytics, third-party scripts, or upload endpoint are included.

    What formats can I use?+

    JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, and BMP can be opened if your browser can decode them. Export to WebP, JPG, or PNG. Unsupported browser formats fail safely with a message.

    Will animated GIFs stay animated?+

    No. Animated images are exported as a still image of their first frame. Color and quality can differ because images are decoded and re-encoded.

    Will this increase image quality?+

    No. Resizing and lossy formats can reduce image detail. PNG may be larger than the original; we show the actual exported size, including when it grows.

    Can I use it offline?+

    Yes, after the page is loaded once on HTTPS or localhost, your browser can cache the studio for offline use. You can also host the static files yourself.
